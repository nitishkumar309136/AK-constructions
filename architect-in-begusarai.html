Architect Services

Begusarai Mein Ghar Design Karwana Ho To

Bihar mein aksar naksha banane wala alag, engineer alag aur thekedar alag hota hai. AK Construction mein architect, structure engineer aur interior designer ek saath kaam karte hain — isliye jo map banta hai, wahi site par banta hai.

  • Ghar ka map (2D floor plan / naksha) — plot ke exact naap par, har kamre ka size
  • 3D front elevation — banne se pehle ghar dekhein, rang aur design badlein
  • Vastu planning — main door, rasoi, bedroom, puja ghar ki sahi jagah
  • Structural drawing — column, beam, footing, sariya ka hisaab (Er. Nitish Kumar)
  • Interior design — modular kitchen, false ceiling, wardrobe, TV unit (3D ke saath)
  • Map pass drawings — Nagar Nigam / panchayat approval ke liye
  • Bank estimate — home loan ke liye bank-format cost estimate
