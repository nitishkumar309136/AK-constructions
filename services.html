Services Se Jude Zaroori Sawal-Jawab

Naksha, construction packages, materials aur structural design ke baare mein poori jankari.

Hum 6 complete services dete hain: (1) 2D/3D Vastu Naksha Design, (2) Turnkey House Construction with Material, (3) Modular Kitchen & Interior Design, (4) 3D Front Elevation & Exterior Design, (5) RCC Structural Engineering & Column-Beam Load Calculations, aur (6) Boundary Wall, Driveway, Landscaping & Renovation Work.

Turnkey contract mein neev ki khudai (foundation digging), branded cement (Ultratech/ACC), TMT sariya (Tata Tiscon/Jindal), red bricks, sand, gitty, electrical wiring (Havells/Polycab), CPVC plumbing, designer vitrified tiles, sanitary fittings, doors-windows aur premium weather-shield paint tak sab kuch shamil rehta hai.

Site measurement aur plot orientation analysis ke baad preliminary 2D layout 3-5 din mein aur final 3D photorealistic front elevation render 7-10 din ke andar aapke modifications ke saath ready ho jaata hai.

Haan, hum Nagar Nigam, Nagar Parishad aur municipal authorities ke building by-laws aur mandatory setbacks ke hisaab se approval-ready architectural blueprints, structural stability certificates aur sanction drawings provide karte hain.

AK Construction ke packages: Basic Construction ₹1,750/sqft se shuru, Standard Package ₹2,200/sqft, aur Luxury/Premium Package ₹3,000/sqft tak hota hai. Exact quote free site visit ke baad plot aur finishing requirements ke hisaab se likhit mein diya jaata hai.

Haan, bilkul! Aap hamari individual standalone services jaise sirf 2D Vastu naksha, sirf 3D front elevation, POP false ceiling, modular kitchen ya renovation service bina full construction contract ke bhi hire kar sakte hain.
