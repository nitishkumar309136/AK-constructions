30x40 Plot

30x40 Plot Par Kya Ban Sakta Hai

30x40 (1200 sq ft) ek chauda aur kam gehra plot hai — 30 ft chaudai ki wajah se yahan kamre side-by-side aate hain aur zyada kamron ko seedhi roshni milti hai. Patna, Begusarai aur Munger ki nayi colonies mein ye size kaafi common hai.

Per floor ~950 sq ft built-up milta hai. 30 ft chaudai mein aage car parking aur bagal mein entrance dono aaram se aa jaate hain — isliye 2BHK + parking is plot ka sabse popular option hai.

Design Tips

30x40 Naksha Banate Waqt Dhyan Dein

  • Chaudai zyada aur gehraai kam hai — isliye bedrooms ko peeche aur living-parking ko aage rakhna sabse practical hai.
  • Dono taraf padosi hon tab bhi 30 ft chaudai mein front aur back windows se achhi roshni milti hai.
  • Staircase ko ek kone mein rakhne se upar ka floor alag entrance ke saath kiraye par diya ja sakta hai.
  • East-facing 30x40 mein main door aur car parking dono East/North-East side rakhna Vastu ke hisaab se achha hai.

Sawal Jawab · FAQs

30x40 Naksha — Sawal-Jawab

30x40 = 1,200 sq ft, jo lagbhag 17.6 dhur (0.88 katha) hota hai — Bihar ke aam naap (1 dhur ≈ 68 sq ft, 1 katha ≈ 1361 sq ft) ke hisaab se. Kuch ilaakon mein katha ka naap alag hota hai, isliye apne kewala/khatiyan se confirm karein.

Ek floor par 2BHK + car parking sabse popular hai; bina parking compact 3BHK bhi banta hai. G+1 mein kul 4–5 bedroom tak ho jaate hain.

Lagbhag 950 sq ft per floor built-up maan kar, Begusarai ke rates par sirf ground floor ka kharch Basic mein ~₹16.6 Lakh, Standard mein ~₹20.9 Lakh aur Premium mein ~₹28.5 Lakh aata hai. G+1 (dono floor) Standard quality mein ~₹41.8 Lakh. Patna mein ~8% zyada. Final quote free site visit ke baad likhit mein milta hai.

Dono lagbhag 1200 sq ft ke hain, lekin 30x40 ki chaudai zyada hai, isliye parking aur kamron ki roshni dono behtar milti hai. 20x60 jaise patle-lambe plot mein beech ke kamron ke liye light-shaft zaroori ho jaata hai.

Standard G+1 ghar aam taur par 6–9 mahine mein turnkey finishing ke saath taiyaar hota hai. Exact timeline site visit ke baad agreement mein likhit diya jaata hai.
