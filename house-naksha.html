Sawal Jawab · FAQs

Naksha Aur Plot Naap — Sawal-Jawab

Bihar mein aam taur par 1 katha = 20 dhur ≈ 1361 sq ft maana jaata hai (1 bigha = 20 katha). Lekin kuch zilon aur gaon mein katha ka naap alag hota hai, isliye apne kewala ya khatiyan mein likha naap zaroor dekhein.

1 dhur lagbhag 68 sq ft (68.06 sq ft) hota hai. Jaise 12 dhur ≈ 817 sq ft — yaani lagbhag 20x40 ka plot.

Site measurement ke baad shuruaati 2D layout aam taur par 3–5 din mein ready hota hai, aur 3D front elevation aapke changes ke saath 7–10 din mein mil jaata hai.

Plot ka naap, facing aur photos WhatsApp par bhej kar shuruaati layout discuss ho sakta hai. Lekin final naksha hum site par exact measurement ke baad hi banate hain, taaki construction mein koi galti na ho.

Haan, hum approval ke liye zaroori 2D drawings, setback aur structural details ke saath naksha taiyaar karte hain. Nagar Nigam, Nagar Parishad ya panchayat — jahan aapka plot hai, wahan ke rules ke hisaab se.

Haan — main door, kitchen (Agneya kon), master bedroom (Nairutya kon), puja room aur staircase ki position Vastu ke hisaab se rakhi jaati hai, modern design ke saath.
