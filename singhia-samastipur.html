Singhia Mein Ghar Banane Ke Sawal

AK Construction ke Architect Raushan Kumar aur Structure Designer Er. Nitish Kumar Singhia, Samastipur ke liye ghar ka map (2D naksha), 3D front elevation aur Vastu planning karte hain. Plot ka naap aur facing WhatsApp ya call (+91 87704 18045) par bhejein — shuruaati 2D map 3–5 din mein, 3D design 7–10 din mein milta hai.

Plinth ko pichhli baadh ke paani se upar rakhein, RCC column-beam frame mein ghar banaayein aur bijli ke board unchai par lagaayein. Zarurat ho to stilt (neeche khula) design bhi ek achha option hai.

Material sahit construction lagbhag ₹1,750/sq ft (Basic), ₹2,200/sq ft (Standard) aur ₹3,000/sq ft (Premium) se shuru hota hai. Singhia ke plot ka final rate free site visit ke baad likhit quote mein diya jaata hai.

Haan. Final map aur quote se pehle hamara engineer Singhia mein aapke plot par aakar naap, level aur aas-paas ki sadak/naali dekhta hai. Site visit ke liye WhatsApp ya call karke time le lein.
