Bihar Ke Mausam Ke Liye

Elevation Banate Waqt Dhyan Dein

Sundar dikhne ke saath front ko baarish, dhoop aur dhool bhi jhelni hoti hai.

  • Har khidki ke upar chhajja (sunshade) — Bihar ki tez baarish aur garmi dono se bachav.
  • Bahar weather-shield / exterior emulsion paint — saadharan paint jaldi kaala padta hai.
  • Halke rang (white, beige, grey) garmi kam sokhte hain; gehre rang sirf patti ya highlight mein.
  • Paschim (West) taraf kam glass — dopahar ki dhoop se ghar garam nahi hota.
  • Railing, grill aur gate ka design elevation ke saath hi tay karein, baad mein alag-alag nahi.
