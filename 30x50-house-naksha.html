30x50 Plot

30x50 Plot Par Kya Ban Sakta Hai

30x50 (1500 sq ft) — lagbhag 22 dhur, yaani 1 katha se thoda zyada — Bihar mein 3BHK ghar ke liye sabse balanced plot size hai. Chaudai 30 ft aur gehraai 50 ft hone se ek hi floor par 3 bedroom, alag dining aur car parking — sab aa jaata hai.

Per floor ~1200 sq ft built-up milta hai. Jo parivaar ek hi floor par rehna chahte hain (buzurg maata-pita ke saath), unke liye ye size khaas taur par achha hai — seedhi chadhne ki zarurat hi nahi.

Layout Options

30x50 Naksha Ke Popular Options

  • Ground 3BHK + car parking — single-floor living, 2 toilet (1 attached)
  • Ground 2BHK + bada living/dining + parking — upar baad mein floor badhane ka option
  • G+1 5BHK duplex — neeche 2 bedroom + common area, upar 3 bedroom + family lounge + balcony

Design Tips

30x50 Naksha Banate Waqt Dhyan Dein

  • Single-floor 3BHK mein corridor chhota rakhein — dining ko 'hub' banakar saare kamre wahin se kholne se jagah bachti hai.
  • Foundation ko shuru se G+1 ya G+2 load ke liye design karwayein, taaki baad mein floor badhana safe rahe.
  • Is size mein kitchen ke peeche alag utility/wash area diya ja sakta hai — Bihar ke gharon mein ye bahut kaam aata hai.

Sawal Jawab · FAQs

30x50 Naksha — Sawal-Jawab

30x50 = 1,500 sq ft, jo lagbhag 22 dhur (1.1 katha) hota hai — Bihar ke aam naap (1 dhur ≈ 68 sq ft, 1 katha ≈ 1361 sq ft) ke hisaab se. Kuch ilaakon mein katha ka naap alag hota hai, isliye apne kewala/khatiyan se confirm karein.

Haan, ~1200 sq ft built-up mein 3 bedroom (10'–12'), living, dining, kitchen, 2 toilet aur ek car parking practical roop se aa jaati hai.

Lagbhag 1200 sq ft per floor built-up maan kar, Begusarai ke rates par sirf ground floor ka kharch Basic mein ~₹21 Lakh, Standard mein ~₹26.4 Lakh aur Premium mein ~₹36 Lakh aata hai. G+1 (dono floor) Standard quality mein ~₹52.8 Lakh. Patna mein ~8% zyada. Final quote free site visit ke baad likhit mein milta hai.

Pehle din se hi column-footing aur beam design G+1 ya G+2 load ke hisaab se karwayein aur staircase ki position fix rakhein. Hamare structural designer Er. Nitish Kumar yahi planning karte hain.

Aam taur par 4–5 bedroom — neeche 1–2 bedroom ke saath living, dining, kitchen, aur upar 3 bedroom, family lounge aur balcony.
