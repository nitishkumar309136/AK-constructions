Civil Engineer Kyun Zaroori Hai

Aam thekedar aksar andaze se sariya aur cement lagate hain — ya to kharch badh jaata hai, ya structure kamzor rehta hai. Civil engineer pehle load ka hisaab lagata hai, phir batata hai ki kahan kitna sariya, kitna mota column aur kitni gehri neev chahiye.

  • Structural design — column, beam, footing, slab aur sariya ka poora drawing
  • Earthquake-safe design — Bihar ke earthquake zone ke hisaab se structure
  • Soil check & foundation — mitti dekh kar neev ki gehraai aur size
  • Ghar ka estimate — cement, sariya, eent, baalu ka hisaab aur bank-format cost estimate
  • Online andaza — cost calculator se turant, ya plot details WhatsApp karke shuruaati estimate
  • Site supervision — foundation casting, column alignment aur slab ke sariya par engineer ki jaanch
  • Purane ghar ki jaanch — upar floor badhane se pehle structure safe hai ya nahi
  • Map pass drawings — Nagar Nigam / panchayat approval ke liye structural details
