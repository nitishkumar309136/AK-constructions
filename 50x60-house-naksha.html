50x60 (3000 sq ft) — lagbhag 44 dhur ya 2.2 katha — bade parivaar ya luxury ghar ke liye plot hai. Neeche ka sample naksha isi size ka hai: 2400 sq ft built-up mein 4BHK, 4 toilet, family lounge, alag living room, dining, utility, puja room, garden aur 2 car parking.

Design Tips

50x60 Naksha Banate Waqt Dhyan Dein

  • Sample mein 4 bedroom chaaron taraf aur family lounge beech mein hai — har kamre ki privacy bani rehti hai.
  • 17 ft jaise bade span ke kamron ke liye beam size aur column grid ka structural calculation zaroori hai — andaze se nahi.
  • 2 car parking ke liye kam se kam 17 ft chaudai rakhein — sample mein yahi rakha gaya hai.

50x60 Naksha — Sawal-Jawab

50x60 = 3,000 sq ft, jo lagbhag 44.1 dhur (2.2 katha) hota hai — Bihar ke aam naap (1 dhur ≈ 68 sq ft, 1 katha ≈ 1361 sq ft) ke hisaab se. Kuch ilaakon mein katha ka naap alag hota hai, isliye apne kewala/khatiyan se confirm karein.

Single floor par hi 4BHK luxury ghar ban jaata hai, jo buzurgon ke liye aasaan hai. G+1 tab chunein jab 6+ bedroom chahiye ya upar ka floor kiraye par dena ho.

Lagbhag 2400 sq ft per floor built-up maan kar, Begusarai ke rates par sirf ground floor ka kharch Basic mein ~₹42 Lakh, Standard mein ~₹52.8 Lakh aur Premium mein ~₹72 Lakh aata hai.
