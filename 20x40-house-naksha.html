20x40 Plot

20x40 Plot Par Kya Ban Sakta Hai

20x40 (800 sq ft) Bihar ke shehri mohallon ka sabse aam plot size hai — Begusarai town, Barauni aur Patna ki colonies mein aksar yahi milta hai. Plot sirf 20 feet chauda hai, isliye naksha banate waqt har foot ka sahi istemaal zaroori hai: seedhi kahan jaayegi, beech ke kamron mein roshni-hawa kaise aayegi, aur gaadi rakhne ki jagah bachegi ya nahi.

Is plot par aam taur par 600–700 sq ft per floor built-up milta hai (front open space aur light-shaft chhod kar). Zyada tar families yahan G+1 banati hain — neeche 1–2BHK aur upar 2BHK, ya poora ek duplex.

Layout Options

20x40 Naksha Ke Popular Options

  • Ground floor 2BHK (bina car parking) — living, 2 bedroom, kitchen, 1 common toilet, seedhi bagal ki deewar ke saath
  • Ground 1BHK + car/bike parking — upar first floor par 2BHK; kiraye par dene ke liye bhi upyogi
  • G+1 duplex 3BHK — neeche living, kitchen aur 1 bedroom; upar 2 bedroom + family lounge

Design Tips

20x40 Naksha Banate Waqt Dhyan Dein

  • 20 ft chaudai mein aam taur par side windows nahi milti (dono taraf padosi) — isliye beech mein 4' × 6' ka light-shaft ya duct rakhna chahiye.
  • Staircase ko lambi deewar ke saath rakhne se kamron ki chaudai kam nahi hoti.
  • Kitchen aur toilet ki plumbing ek hi line mein rakhne se kharch aur leakage dono kam hote hain.
  • Car parking chahiye to ground par 1BHK hi practical hai — 20 ft mein se 9–10 ft parking le leti hai.

Sawal Jawab · FAQs

20x40 Naksha — Sawal-Jawab

20x40 = 800 sq ft, jo lagbhag 11.8 dhur (0.59 katha) hota hai — Bihar ke aam naap (1 dhur ≈ 68 sq ft, 1 katha ≈ 1361 sq ft) ke hisaab se. Kuch ilaakon mein katha ka naap alag hota hai, isliye apne kewala/khatiyan se confirm karein.

Ek floor par aaram se 2BHK (bina car parking) ya 1BHK + parking banta hai. G+1 mein 3BHK duplex, ya neeche-upar alag-alag 2 units — dono common options hain.

Lagbhag 650 sq ft per floor built-up maan kar, Begusarai ke rates par sirf ground floor ka kharch Basic mein ~₹11.4 Lakh, Standard mein ~₹14.3 Lakh aur Premium mein ~₹19.5 Lakh aata hai. G+1 (dono floor) Standard quality mein ~₹28.6 Lakh. Patna mein ~8% zyada. Final quote free site visit ke baad likhit mein milta hai.

Haan, lekin tab ground floor par 1BHK ya chhota 2BHK hi banega, kyunki 20 ft mein se 9–10 ft parking le leti hai. Stilt parking (neeche sirf parking, upar ghar) bhi ek option hai.

Vastu mein East aur North facing plot aasaan maane jaate hain, lekin South ya West facing plot par bhi sahi main-door position, kitchen (Agneya kon) aur master bedroom (Nairutya kon) rakh kar achha Vastu-anukool naksha banta hai.
