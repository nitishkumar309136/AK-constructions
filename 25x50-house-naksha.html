25x50 Plot

25x50 Plot Par Kya Ban Sakta Hai

25x50 (1250 sq ft) plot lagbhag 18 dhur — yaani qareeb 1 katha — ka hota hai, aur Begusarai, Samastipur, Khagaria jaise shehron mein naye ghar ke liye bahut pasand kiya jaata hai. Plot lamba hai, isliye kamre aage se peeche ek line mein aate hain, aur 25 ft chaudai mein parking ke saath bhi theek-thaak kamre nikal aate hain.

Aam taur par ~1000 sq ft per floor built-up milta hai. Ek floor par 2BHK + car parking, ya bina parking 3BHK; G+1 mein 4BHK duplex bhi aaram se banta hai.

Layout Options

25x50 Naksha Ke Popular Options

  • Ground 2BHK + car parking — aage parking aur verandah, beech mein living-dining, peeche 2 bedroom
  • Ground 3BHK (bina parking) — joint family ke liye; ek bedroom attached toilet ke saath
  • G+1 4BHK duplex — neeche living, kitchen, dining, guest room; upar 3 bedroom + balcony

Design Tips

25x50 Naksha Banate Waqt Dhyan Dein

  • Lambe plot mein beech ke dining/staircase area ke upar skylight ya duct rakhein — warna din mein bhi light jalani padti hai.
  • Staircase ko beech mein rakhne se aage aur peeche ke hisse zarurat padne par alag unit ki tarah bhi use ho sakte hain.
  • Kitchen ko South-East (Agneya) aur master bedroom ko South-West (Nairutya) mein rakhna is size mein aasaan hai.
  • Peeche 4–5 ft khula chhodne se back rooms mein cross-ventilation milta hai.

Sawal Jawab · FAQs

25x50 Naksha — Sawal-Jawab

25x50 = 1,250 sq ft, jo lagbhag 18.4 dhur (0.92 katha) hota hai — Bihar ke aam naap (1 dhur ≈ 68 sq ft, 1 katha ≈ 1361 sq ft) ke hisaab se. Kuch ilaakon mein katha ka naap alag hota hai, isliye apne kewala/khatiyan se confirm karein.

Ek floor par 2BHK + car parking ya bina parking 3BHK. G+1 duplex mein 4BHK tak aaram se nikal aata hai.

Lagbhag 1000 sq ft per floor built-up maan kar, Begusarai ke rates par sirf ground floor ka kharch Basic mein ~₹17.5 Lakh, Standard mein ~₹22 Lakh aur Premium mein ~₹30 Lakh aata hai. G+1 (dono floor) Standard quality mein ~₹44 Lakh. Patna mein ~8% zyada. Final quote free site visit ke baad likhit mein milta hai.

Haan — 25x50 duplex ke liye achha size hai. Neeche common area aur upar private bedrooms rakhne se 4BHK tak ban jaata hai. Foundation shuru se usi hisaab se design ho to aage floor badhana bhi aasaan rehta hai.

Haan, hum Bihar Building Byelaws ke setback aur FAR rules ke hisaab se drawing banate hain. Road ki chaudai aur plot ki location ke hisaab se khula chhodne wali jagah badalti hai, jo hum site visit par check karte hain.
