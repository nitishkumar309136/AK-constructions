Home Loan Ke Baare Mein Aksar Puche Jaane Wale Sawal

Bank loan documents, cost estimate, stage-wise release aur EMI se judi jankari.

Nahi. AK Construction ek certified construction company hai, bank nahi. Hum home construction loan ke liye zaroori documents (approved 2D naksha, certified structural drawings aur bank-format detailed cost estimate) taiyaar karte hain aur leading bank/NBFC partners (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, PNB) se connect karwane mein poori madad karte hain.

Bank mein aam taur par: (1) Zameen ki Registry / Khatiyan / Mutation papers, (2) KYC documents (Aadhar Card, PAN Card), (3) Income Proof (Salary slips/Form 16 ya ITR with business proof), aur (4) Architect & Civil Engineer dwara certified 2D house plan aur detailed construction cost estimate chahiye hota hai.

Bank loan amount ka calculation zameen par hone wale construction cost ke aadhar par karta hai. Certified architect aur structural engineer ka detailed estimate hone se bank ko project ki authenticity confirm hoti hai aur loan sanction process bina kisi rukawat ke fast complete hota hai.

Home construction loan ka paisa bank ek saath nahi balki work stages ke completion par release karta hai (jaise Plinth level, Slab casting, Brickwork & Plaster, aur Finishing stage). AK Construction har stage par bank engineer verification ke liye certification aur stage-wise progress reports provide karta hai.

Construction ke dauran bank aam taur par Pre-EMI (sirf disbursed amount par interest) leta hai. Full EMI ghar complete hone ke baad ya moratorium period khatam hone par shuru hoti hai.

Haan, kyunki AK Construction ek GST-registered firm hai aur hum legal agreement, stamp-paper quotation aur standard engineering drawings dete hain jisse banks aur NBFCs loan file ko jaldi approve karte hain.
