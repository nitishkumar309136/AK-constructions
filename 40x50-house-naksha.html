Design Tips

40x50 Naksha Banate Waqt Dhyan Dein

  • Sample naksha mein master bedroom South-West (Nairutya) mein aur kitchen-puja East side mein hain — Vastu ke hisaab se balanced arrangement.
  • Garden aage rakhne se ghar road se thoda peeche aata hai — dhool aur shor dono kam.
  • Utility area kitchen ke bagal mein — kapde dhona, bartan aur store sab ek jagah.

Sawal Jawab · FAQs

40x50 Naksha — Sawal-Jawab

40x50 = 2,000 sq ft, jo lagbhag 29.4 dhur (1.47 katha) hota hai — Bihar ke aam naap (1 dhur ≈ 68 sq ft, 1 katha ≈ 1361 sq ft) ke hisaab se. Kuch ilaakon mein katha ka naap alag hota hai, isliye apne kewala/khatiyan se confirm karein.

Ek floor par 3BHK + garden + parking (sample naksha jaisa) ya garden chhota karke 4BHK. G+1 mein 6 bedroom tak, ya do alag 3BHK units.

Lagbhag 1500 sq ft per floor built-up maan kar, Begusarai ke rates par sirf ground floor ka kharch Basic mein ~₹26.2 Lakh, Standard mein ~₹33 Lakh aur Premium mein ~₹45 Lakh aata hai. G+1 (dono floor) Standard quality mein ~₹66 Lakh. Patna mein ~8% zyada. Final quote free site visit ke baad likhit mein milta hai.

Sample sirf reference ke liye hai. Hum aapke plot ke exact naap, facing, road aur parivaar ki zarurat ke hisaab se naya naksha banate hain — sample ko base maan kar changes bhi karwa sakte hain.

Haan, built-up thoda kam (~1300–1400 sq ft) rakhne par ya parking ko 17–18 ft chauda karne par 2 car aur chhota garden dono aa jaate hain.
