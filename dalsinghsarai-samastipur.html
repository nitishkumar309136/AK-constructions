Dalsinghsarai Mein Ghar Banane Ke Sawal

AK Construction ke Architect Raushan Kumar aur Structure Designer Er. Nitish Kumar Dalsinghsarai, Samastipur ke liye ghar ka map (2D naksha), 3D front elevation aur Vastu planning karte hain. Plot ka naap aur facing WhatsApp ya call (+91 87704 18045) par bhejein — shuruaati 2D map 3–5 din mein, 3D design 7–10 din mein milta hai.

Zameen ke kaagzaat, plot ka naap aur setback rules ke hisaab se 2D drawings (floor plan, section, elevation) aur structural details. Hum yeh drawings approval ke liye taiyaar karte hain; exact documents Nagar Parishad office ki suchi ke hisaab se milaye jaate hain.

Material sahit construction lagbhag ₹1,750/sq ft (Basic), ₹2,200/sq ft (Standard) aur ₹3,000/sq ft (Premium) se shuru hota hai. Dalsinghsarai ke plot ka final rate free site visit ke baad likhit quote mein diya jaata hai.

Haan. Final map aur quote se pehle hamara engineer Dalsinghsarai mein aapke plot par aakar naap, level aur aas-paas ki sadak/naali dekhta hai. Site visit ke liye WhatsApp ya call karke time le lein.
